## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-19

First version.

### Added

- A 3D room with an oak cube shelf that grows with your library: records stand in the cubes in
  sort order, lean when a cube is not full, with A–Z (or decade, or genre) divider cards. Spare
  cubes hold a plant, a speaker, books or a print.
- Your music folder as albums: File System Access where the browser has it (the folder is kept),
  a folder picker everywhere else, or drag and drop. Tags from MP3, FLAC, M4A, Ogg, WAV and more
  (music-metadata); folder names when there are none; two-disc folders as one album; compilations.
- Covers from `cover.jpg` and friends, then from the files' own art, then drawn by the page in one
  of seven printed-sleeve styles. Spines take their colours from the cover.
- Pull a record out to see its cover; turn it over for the tracklist by side.
- Play: the record slides out of its sleeve onto the turntable on top of the shelf, and the album
  goes to one of six vinyl players beside the shelf (or in a tab) over the vinyl-shelf/1
  protocol. The player plays it without touching its own saved library.
- Search, five sort orders, a random pick, a list view, daylight and dusk, keyboard control,
  phone layout, reduced motion.
- A demo shelf of 150 made-up records with synthesized tunes.
- One self-contained `index.html`: the same file is the website and the page you double-click.
