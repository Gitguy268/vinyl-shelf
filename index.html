Arrow keys move along the shelf. Enter pulls the record out. Slash searches. R picks a record at random. L shows the list.

Vinyl Shelf

Your music, as records

Put your music folder on a shelf.

Every album becomes a record with its own sleeve. Pull one out, turn it over, and play it on one of six vinyl players.

Everything happens in this browser tab. Your music is read from your computer and never uploaded.

    Click the player once to start the sound. This browser keeps it quiet until then.

    Your shelf is here. Connect the music folder again to play.
